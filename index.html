The story

The Heron Bay Hoodlums started the way the best things on the coast start — young. Carl Peters and a couple of his cousins started the clique as young boys in Heron Bay, Alabama, a fishing community where the boats go out before the sun and a man's word is his bond. Their mama used to shoo them out of the house — "Y'all get out of here, you hooligans" — and the name stuck. That was some forty years ago, and it has held ever since.

HBH was never about trouble. It is about a place — the bay, the docks, the best fishermen on the coast — and folks who never forgot where they came from. People down in Heron Bay are proud to be from Heron Bay. This line carries that pride.

The line itself grew out of an idea to bring HBH to nautical and coastal gear online, built under Still Standing — the same fisherman cross that stands over all of this work. Credit where it's due: the clique is Carl and his cousins'. And what grows from this line is meant to be shared — the benefits and the blessings of it are for everybody back home, not one man.

This brand is being brought in by James Bosarge — together with his hooligan brothers and sisters from Heron Bay, Bayou La Batre, Mobile, and Dauphin Island, Alabama. Thank you, all of you — and thank everyone who has stood with us along the way. As his mother would say, it is out of love for all things and everyone and everything. May they all be blessed.
