🎣 The fishing index — by the phases of the moon

The old hands have always fished by the moon. Fish feed hardest around the major periods (when the moon is straight overhead and straight underfoot) and the minor periods (moonrise and moonset) — and the days around the new moon and full moon run strongest. 🦪 Oyster season — OPEN now, and Heron Bay is one of the grids

Alabama's oyster season opened Monday, November 2, 2026 (Alabama Marine Resources Division). Harvest is limited to three grids — Cedar Point East, Cedar Point West, and Heron Bay — Monday through Friday, 7 a.m. to 2 p.m. The closing date isn't fixed: MRD sets it from daily harvest reports, and grids can close as the season runs — populations on the public reefs are still low, so the Division is watching landings close.

Before you go: check exactly which areas inside the grids are open on MRD's grid map, and report in at the Oyster Management Station (16871 Dauphin Island Parkway, Coden) as the rules require. The Division's word is the one that counts.
