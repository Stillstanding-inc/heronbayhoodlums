Boaters warning boaters

This board is for the working water — Bayou La Batre, Fowl River, Portersville Bay, Mobile Bay, Petit Bois Pass and the ramps and runs between. See something that could hurt the next man — a submerged log, debris in the channel, a broken day-marker, a ramp tore up or blocked? Post it. Dead in the water and it's not an emergency? Post it, and let the closest boat find you. Got a boat and a willing hand? Say that here too.
